How OpenCode Go works

You pay $10/month flat

Go is a subscription, not pay-per-token. You get one API key that talks to every model in the lineup. No juggling provider accounts.

There are three hard caps

Usage is metered in dollars: $12 per rolling 5-hour window, $30 per week, $60 per month. When a cap is hit, requests are rejected until it resets.

Each model has its own budget

Premium models (Grok, Kimi K3, GPT 5.6 Luna, etc.) are limited to $15/month; some mid-tier models to $30/month; standard open coders to the full $60/month. The page shows you this on every row.

"Requests per 5h" is your real budget

Official estimates based on observed token patterns: a Kimi K3 call costs ~$0.07/request so you get ~110 requests/5h. A DeepSeek V4 Flash call costs ~$0.0015 so you get ~7,600. Mix cheap and premium models to make the most of the cap.

Privacy varies by model

Most Go models list zero-day retention. The closed ones (Grok, GPT 5.6 Luna) retain logs for 30 days. The Muse Contributor tier can use your prompts for training and is region-limited.

How OpenCode Zen works

You pay per 1M tokens, no subscription

Zen is pure pay-as-you-go. Top up your balance once and use any model — there is no monthly fee and no minimum spend.

Auto-reload keeps the balance topped up

If your balance drops below $5, Zen reloads $20 automatically. You can change the amount or disable auto-reload entirely. Credit-card processing fees are passed through at cost.

Cached reads and writes are cheaper

Most models bill cached reads at a fraction of the input price (e.g. Claude Opus 4.8: $5.00 input vs $0.50 cached read). Long sessions that reuse context save real money.

You can set monthly limits per workspace and per member

Useful for teams. With auto-reload on, a single low-balance moment can push a month above the cap — disable it if you need a hard ceiling.

Privacy is mostly zero-retention

Models are hosted in the US. Most providers do not retain or train on your data. Exceptions: OpenAI and Anthropic retain requests for 30 days; the Free models and the Muse Contributor tier do train on your prompts.
